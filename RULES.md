# How to play Yahtzee

Take turns rolling five dice. Fill one box on your scorecard each turn. After everyone has filled all 13 boxes, the highest total wins. [Hasbro's official Yahtzee instructions](https://www.hasbro.com/common/instruct/yahtzee.pdf)

## Take a turn

1. Roll all five dice.
2. You can stop and score now or roll again. Keep any dice you like and reroll the rest. You can change which dice you keep before the next roll.
3. You get up to three rolls in total. When you stop, pick one unused box and record your points. A zero still fills the box.

## Score your dice

**Upper section.** Pick a number from Ones through Sixes and add only the dice showing that number. For example, if you roll **2, 2, 2, 4, 6**, you can record **6 points in Twos**. An upper-section subtotal of **63 or more** earns **35 bonus points**.

**Lower section.** Use the table to see what each combination is worth. An unused category you don't qualify for can be filled with zero.

| Category | What you need | Points |
|---|---|---:|
| Three of a kind | At least three dice match | Add all five dice |
| Four of a kind | At least four dice match | Add all five dice |
| Full house | Three of one number and two of another | 25 |
| Small straight | Four different numbers in a row, such as 2–3–4–5 | 30 |
| Large straight | Five different numbers in a row | 40 |
| Yahtzee | All five dice match | 50 |
| Chance | Any roll | Add all five dice |

## Official extra Yahtzee and Joker rules

If your Yahtzee box already contains **50**, each additional Yahtzee earns **100 bonus points**. If the box contains **0**, you still use the Joker rules for that roll.

1. Fill the matching upper box if it is open. Five fours would score 20 in Fours.
2. If that box is filled, choose an open lower box. A Joker can earn the listed points for a full house or either straight. Three of a kind, four of a kind, and Chance use the dice total.
3. If all lower boxes are filled too, record zero in an open upper box. [Hasbro's extra Yahtzee and Joker rules](https://hasbro-new.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/211/)

Yahtzee2.0 uses regular category scoring and the upper-section bonus. The official extra Yahtzee bonus and Joker rules are currently unimplemented in the app.

## Play in Yahtzee2.0

Select **Roll Dice** to start. Click a die to hold it or click it again to release it. The **Preview** column shows what you could score. Select a row and click **Record score** to end your turn. For Mac and Windows launch instructions, see the [README](README.md#run-the-game).
